Microsoft Fabric ETL Developer at Tantus Solutions Group Inc., Edmonton, AB, working on the PCL Construction account

Tantus Solutions Group Inc. · Client: Canadian Western Bank

Regulatory & AML pipelines that hit SLA for three years

ETL Developer / Data Consultant at Tantus Solutions Group Inc., Edmonton, AB, working on the Canadian Western Bank account

The challenge

A regulated bank depended on accurate, on-time data for AML, KYC, CDD, EDD, SAR and FINTRAC regulatory reporting, transaction monitoring and credit risk. That meant high-volume feeds from ACH, ATM, EFT, wires, POS, cheques and billing, all while modernizing from SAS 9 to SAS Viya.

What I did

  • Owned every incremental ETL pipeline across core banking, AML and regulatory datasets.
  • Engineered CDC and SCD Type 2 pipelines processing 20M+ transaction records per batch cycle across AML, KYC, ATS, Loans and Credit Card portfolios.
  • Wrote 50+ Base SAS and SAS Macro diagnostic and remediation scripts to fix recurring data quality, reconciliation and root-cause defects.
  • Built test-driven pipelines with SQL profiling and checksum validation in stored procedures.
  • Automated ETL workflows end to end and governed CI/CD releases across Dev, Test and Prod.
  • Authored the SAS/SQL package standard adopted team-wide across 150+ package migrations from SAS 9.3 to SAS Viya, and supported a SAS 9.4 to Viya 3.5/4 migration proof-of-concept.
  • Enabled real-time access to core banking systems through Denodo data virtualization, bridging legacy SAS and cloud platforms without moving data.
  • Delivered ad hoc curated datasets and visualizations in SAS Enterprise Guide for business and compliance requests.

Results

  • 99.5%SLA compliance over three years
  • ~95%Repeat defects eliminated
  • −45%Production defects
  • −50%Release failures
  • −25%Manual effort & integration errors
